Sotto voice window: UI polish

Before (origin/main) and after (feat/ui-polish), light and dark, at 420×640, 360×420 and 640×900, rendered at 2× in the desktop app's layout (html.host-app, 28 px title-bar inset). How they were made: the real web/index.html + styles.css + dial.js + lib.js in silent headless Chrome (test/helpers/silent-chrome.js), with app.js replaced by an empty module and each state written into the DOM over CDP the way app.js renders it (as in design/AUDIT.md and test/web/layout.test.js). No daemon or OpenAI session. The caption panel is not refitted without app.js, so a partly clipped caption line in some frames is an artifact of the harness, in both columns.

What changed: cool neutral light theme with no warm washes (the amber banner and approval fill read as beige), tones carried by icons and rings; shadows instead of hard 1 px borders on buttons, cards and pop-ups; macOS control sizes (36 px push buttons, 32 px in banners, 13 px labels) with hit areas of at least 40 px; concentric radii; a quieter usage capsule and more room in the header; a calmer dial at rest on light; icon crossfades (scale 0.25→1, opacity, blur 4 px→0); staggered enters and short exits for banners, cards and the drawer, none on first load.
