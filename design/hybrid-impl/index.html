Filament, with the best of Orrery

The 0.3.4 panel was a dial with a status word and a boxed Claude card. 0.4.0 keeps Filament's layout (one string across the panel, Claude's words on a serif page, fixed zones) and adds Orrery's moments on that string. The native app and the web page draw the same panel.

  • One lit string instead of the dial. Your peg on the left is the mute control. The string plucks when you talk and sheds tides when Sotto talks. At rest it draws nothing.
  • Claude gets a column, not a card. Its words are set in the serif at reading size. The row above shows a moon-phase glyph and a fixed-width timer, and the headline is Claude's news ("Approve in the terminal", "Claude finished").
  • Approvals are an eclipse. Claude's bead slides back into your peg, a gold corona comes up and the page frames the command in mono with the reason, the wait timer and Show terminal. Gold appears only while an approval is live.
  • Finished steps leave stars on the string, and a sleeping peg shows its night side. Waking is a sunrise.
  • Nothing moves. The header status sits in a fixed slot, the capsule is one flat pill with symbols, and every zone keeps its edges across states. Notices sit on the caption line instead of floating over Claude's page.
  • Quieter controls. Pause and power are icon buttons in the footer next to the persona and voice, and the outlined Pause and End voice buttons are gone.

Sizes

The compact strip (native, 320 × 96), the large panel and the small web window. Zones keep their order at every size.

Quiet states

States that must not look broken: sleeping, paused, the daily cap, setup and errors. Claude's page stays readable underneath and the state is written on the caption line.

Reduce Motion

With Reduce Motion on, the string holds still, the corona is a still ring and every state stays distinct.
