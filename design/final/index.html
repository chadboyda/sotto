Voice window: Instrument dial + quiet text

The hybrid the user chose: the multi-ring instrument from direction A around the mute button, with the simple text area from direction C, rebased onto main with idle sleep / voice wake (#6) and the Sotto desktop app (#7). Every screenshot is the real web/ page (app.js, dial.js, lib.js, wake.js) at 2x, driven over CDP in headless Chrome against a fake daemon and a fake mic/WebRTC layer; no OpenAI session and not the user's daemon. States are 420x640 unless noted; each pair is dark then light. The harness checked all 132 renders: 0 with page scroll, 0 with a clipped or off-screen action.

  • Inner ring, you: 48 short, thick capsules driven by the mic spectrum (jagged, asymmetric) with peak-hold dots. Muted: they collapse into violet dots and the button fills violet. Sleeping: they show what the local wake detector hears, and breathe slowly.
  • Channel: an empty band of at least 0.05 R (pinned by a test) so the two voices never merge into one sunburst.
  • Outer ring, Sotto: a continuous, mirror-symmetric filled envelope with a crisp edge over 144 hairlines, from an unplayed track.clone() of the remote voice; the <audio> element stays the echo-cancellation reference. In light mode its ink is a cool steel (#3B5170), not the near-black text colour.
  • Bezel: 120 fine ticks that ratchet one tick a second while someone talks. Claude Code working lights a chase of 16 bezel ticks in blue that travels round (dimmed and stopped while the voice connection is down). Rim: grey sweep while connecting, dashed amber pulse while Claude waits for approval, amber tick at 10 o'clock toward Chrome's mic bubble.
  • Bloom: a pre-drawn halo whose opacity follows the loudest talker: a soft glow in dark mode, a pale tint wash in light mode.
  • Reduced motion: levels snap to rest/mid/full, CSS motion stops, shapes still differ. Shape, word and colour always change together.

What changed in the critique round

  • Rings differ by form (capsules vs filled envelope) with a clear channel; light-mode bloom is a tint, the assistant ink is steel.
  • Captions: exactly two lines, no fade mask; a line that does not fit is dropped whole, and a too-long latest line keeps its newest words.
  • Header: whole items hide by priority (status, usage, project); the money is never cut. The session clock moved under the dial.
  • Approval: the hero word becomes "Approve in the terminal" in amber; the floor state moves to the small line. At small heights the card keeps "Approve or deny it in the terminal."
  • Sleeping is its own state (crescent glyph, wake.js copy, inner ring = wake detector, Wake now as a secondary button).
  • The dial scales with the window (about 200 px at 640 tall, 340 px at 900) and every card keeps a dormant dial above its copy.
  • Cards: only the middle scrolls; the title and the action always show (checked at 360x420 for every state).
  • Screen readers: card changes are announced (errors, blocked mic and the cap via role=alert); mute/unmute are announced on the acknowledgement; focus moves to the card action instead of falling to the page.
  • Space mutes even after a mouse click on a button; M works with the settings drawer open (and from the app's menu bar).
  • Autoplay banner stays until audio plays, with a Turn on audio button; banners that say "try again" get the button.
  • Inside the Sotto app the mic copy names macOS and Sotto, never Chrome, and has no pointer.
  • createDial is guarded; dial.js runs without createConicGradient (WebKit before 16.4).

Header polish (review round 2)

Feedback: the status bar was plain grey text at one size and hard to read, and "14.2 min" is not how anyone says a duration. Now it is two groups on the 4/8 grid. Left: the status glyph and the state word (15/600 in the state's ink), with a quieter 12 px line under it for the detail ("Still billing" in violet while muted) and the project. Right: today's usage as one sunken readout, figures in the strong ink and units quieter, tabular numerals, then the gear (44 px target). Durations are whole and human everywhere: "under a minute", "14 min", "1 hr 5 min", "2 hr"; money is "$0.71" ("<$0.01" for a sliver). The reading holds for 10 s unless the minute changes, so the cents do not flicker. Under the dial: "session just started" / "14 min this session". Paused: "Paused after 3 minutes of silence" (never "2.5 minutes"). The daily-cap card says how much was used ("You've used today's 2 hours of voice."). /talk status and /talk off: "31 min today ($1.56)".

When the row is short of room: the project ellipsizes, then hides, then the detail, then the word "today"; the state word and the money never go. Contrast (WCAG 2.x): lowest pair is light-mode green "Live" on the page at 4.96:1; units on the readout 7.1:1 (light) / 9.4:1 (dark).
